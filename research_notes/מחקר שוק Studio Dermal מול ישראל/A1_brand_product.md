**Background on the mechanism (not researched here; needs sources from the mechanism/competitor researcher before it is presented as fact)**
- [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE] Instant tighteners in this class (PTR Instant FIRMx is the best-known) use mineral film formers such as sodium silicate and/or magnesium aluminum silicate, sometimes with polymer film formers. As the water evaporates, the film dries and contracts on the skin surface. That tension physically flattens soft puffiness, fine lines and crepey texture for some hours. The effect is mechanical and does not change the skin.
- [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE] Common failure modes:
  - a white or grey cast or visible "frosting", especially when applied too thickly or on deeper skin tones;
  - cracking or flaking with expression or over dry skin;
  - breakdown when layered over moisturizer, or under oily or creamy concealer;
  - tightness or stinging, because silicate solutions are alkaline;
  - the need to stay still and not touch the area while it sets.
- [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE] Who it tends to suit: soft, fluid or fat puffiness ("bags"), fine lines and crepey texture, and crow's feet in people who can apply a thin layer and set makeup lightly over it. Who it suits poorly:
  - deep folds and hollows or tear troughs (tightening can exaggerate a hollow);
  - dark circles caused by pigment or blood vessels (a tint is the only help);
  - very dry or flaky skin, and deeper skin tones (residue shows);
  - hooded or very mobile skin;
  - people who need it to last through heat, sweat or tears.
