## 6. AliExpress, Temu and Shein: instant eye-bag / eye-tightener listings, prices, ratings, delivery time to Israel

Cross-border instant eye-bag and tightening creams are plentiful and very cheap. AliExpress listings run about $1.95 to $6.94 (about ₪6 to ₪26), and one popular 30 ml listing costs $2.02. Temu and AliExpress both run Hebrew storefronts, and AliExpress has a Hebrew page for a "Peter Thomas Roth Eye Tightener". Delivery to Israel takes about 20 days from Temu and 7 to 15 business days from Shein. No AliExpress figure was returned, but Globes reported "60 days per parcel" during the war. ### Inferences
Offer anchors for the Israeli launch (all [INFERENCE], built on the table above)
- Single-unit price: about ₪129 to ₪149 fits the open band. It sits above L'Oréal's ₪111 ceiling, so the product is not judged as "another pharmacy cream", and well below PTR (₪198) and IL MAKIAGE (₪319). Pricing at Studio Dermal's dollar level (about ₪96 to ₪111) would put it on L'Oréal's shelf price against a brand Israel Hayom has already tested negatively. Pricing at ₪199 or more would compete with PTR's and IL MAKIAGE's brand trust.
- Bundles: 2 units at about ₪229 to ₪249 with free shipping; 3 units at about ₪299 to ₪319. Three units would then cost about what one IL MAKIAGE Power Rewind costs on sale. That is a useful internal anchor, but naming the competitor in ads needs legal review.
- Shipping: about ₪19 to ₪29 on a single unit is market-normal (IL MAKIAGE ₪21 below ₪299; Zap retailers free to ₪35). Free shipping from 2 units.
- Delivery promise: 1 to 3 business days. This beats Temu (about 20 days), Shein (7 to 15 business days) and Zap retailers' 3 to 14 business days, and matches IL MAKIAGE's same/next-day dispatch.
- Payment: Bit, card and PayPal are the minimum because IL MAKIAGE offers all three. Installments (2 to 3 payments) for bundles are an Israeli norm per B4 [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE there], but no competitor's installment terms were verified.
- Guarantee: at least 14 days money-back covering opened product, to match IL MAKIAGE's Power Rewind trial. A 30-day guarantee would beat every verified Israeli competitor.
- Compare-at pricing: IL MAKIAGE shows a strikethrough (₪399 to ₪319). A ₪249 to ₪299 compare-at next to ₪129 to ₪149 would be in line with market practice only if it reflects a real former price. Israeli reference-price rules were not checked in this round (see B4 / A10).
- Medical anchors: present clinic prices only as price context ("less than one Botox session"), never as equivalent results, given the B4 claim risks.
