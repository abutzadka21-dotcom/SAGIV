## 1. Which additional direct competitors exist, and what are their profiles? (STEP 3)

### Takeaway
Round 2 adds 9 sourced direct competitors to round 1's 6, for 15 in total: Il Makiage Power Rewind Eye Tightener, L'Oréal Paris Revitalift Eye Bag Instant Eraser (US) / Laser Eye Bag Eraser (UK), My Perfect Eyes, StriVectin Hyperlift Eye Instant Eye Fix, IT Cosmetics Bye Bye Under Eye Bags, goPure Instant Lift Eye Gel, PrimeLift Instant Eye Tightener, HealthyDerm Instant Tightening Eye Serum and Hydroxatone Instant Effect 90 Second Wrinkle Reducer. Averyard moved from a bare title to a profile, and it is a near-twin of TMP-02 (20 mL, peptide + seaweed, 3-7 minutes, pea-sized dose) sold by a multi-niche dropship store. Four starting-list brands are not direct competitors: Kleem Organics and LilyAna (treatment creams), Revitol (treatment cream with "instant" wording) and Elizabeth Grant (hybrid). The main change since round 1 is that beauty conglomerates now sell temporary eye-bag tighteners on the UK high street (L'Oréal Paris at Boots, Superdrug and Tesco for £12.82-£29.99 per 15 ml; IT Cosmetics and StriVectin at Boots). Studio Dermal's $29.99 for 0.67 oz ($1.51/ml) undercuts the PTR Easy-Wear SKU it copies ($39.00 for the same 0.67 oz, $1.97/ml) by 23%, while its $79.99 compare-at is about twice the leader's real shelf price. Profile depth is uneven: prices are missing for StriVectin, IT Cosmetics, L'Oréal US, PrimeLift, HealthyDerm and Averyard, and no round-2 entity has a captured review count.

2. [INFERENCE] Studio Dermal copies the leader's newest SKU in name ("Temporary Eye Tightener"), size (0.67 oz), study framing ("39 women") and wear claims (tint, makeup-compatible). Its $29.99 is only 23% under PTR Easy-Wear's $39.00 and is matched by eCosmetics' $29.25 auto-replenish price, so the "dupe discount" is thin. 3. [INFERENCE] In the UK, a shopper can buy a L'Oréal eye-bag eraser at Boots, Superdrug or Tesco, often for £13-£17 online. That is at or below Studio Dermal's £19.99 Instagram price (round 1). The same L'Oréal product is the incumbent in Israeli pharmacies (B1).
4. [INFERENCE] White-label convergence: Averyard and Studio Dermal share size, actives, set time and copy themes; HealthyDerm-style "polysaccharide formula" products and generic "Easy-Wear" Amazon listings (round 1) show the same pattern. An Israeli seller should assume clones of any OEM formula within months and compete on proof, service, guarantee and local trust, not on formula claims.
5. [INFERENCE] Time-to-result claims split by seller type. DTC sellers promise "90 seconds" (Hydroxatone name; Studio Dermal listicle) or "under 2 minutes" (PrimeLift). Large brands state longer, more defensible set times: 15 minutes (L'Oréal) and 20 minutes (IT Cosmetics).
