Method and reading notes (apply to every section):

My one WebFetch attempt (the BuzzFeed test article, chosen because it quotes TikTok commenters) returned "EGRESS_BLOCKED". Everything below comes from search-result titles, captions and the search tool's summaries. No page was opened.
- Tags: [VERIFIED] = the text appears in a search result tied to the cited URL. [VERIFIED, page uncertain] = the search tool returned the text but did not say which of the listed URLs it came from, so the candidate URLs are listed. [PARAPHRASE] = the search tool's own summary wording (copied exactly where it appears in quotation marks), not confirmed as the source page's verbatim text. [INFERENCE] = my own conclusion. [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE] = background with no source found in this session.
- Dates: I decoded TikTok post dates from the video ID (the top 32 bits of a TikTok video ID are the Unix creation time), computed locally. YouTube dates are approximate. I counted back from 2026-10-04 using the "N days ago" values the search tool returned. Both kinds of date are [INFERENCE: computed].
- Time window: the brief covers 2024 to Oct 2026. The moment the category went viral (@trinidad1967, Aug 2021) and several posts that set the template came before 2024. I keep them as context and mark them "pre-window".
- Dashes inside quotation marks are copied exactly as the search tool returned them. In my own text I use "-" or ":".
- I could not retrieve any Studio Dermal or Janets Beauty ad creative. See section 8, "Could not verify".

## 1. Hooks: 20-25+ viral or long-running hooks (verbatim), classified by pattern

### Takeaway
Hooks in this category rest on one mechanism: "watch it happen", plus a number of minutes (2, 3-5, 5 or 10 minutes, "instantly", "in minutes"). Speed appears in 21 of the 71 hook items collected and size-of-claim in 16. Since 2025, brands have added hooks that handle objections ("no white residue", "Under Concealer", "Easy-Wear", "2026 Advanced"). Since late 2025, YouTube has filled with skeptic question hooks ("Is it a GIMMICK?", "DOES IT ACTUALLY WORK?"). No Studio Dermal or Janets Beauty ad hook could be retrieved. The only Studio Dermal-branded wording I found is marketplace listing copy, much of it on look-alike listings.

## 7. Ad style analysis (UGC vs professional and other style dimensions)

### Takeaway
The category runs on two economies. One is lo-fi UGC and affiliate proof (TikTok and YouTube testers). The other is professional direct-response TV with sponsored studio segments (Plexaderm) and founder or brand content (PTR, Skin Rocks). Creative is mostly female, solo, at home, image-led (the visible change is the message), emotional in the hook and logical in the fine print. Dark-side angles (myths, warnings, "don't buy") are a minority used by experts and skeptics. The outliers are a male CEO, a pharmacist, Spanish- and German-language tests, and an anti-category counter-trend.
