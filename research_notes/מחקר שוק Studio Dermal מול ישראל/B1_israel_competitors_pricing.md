## 1. Does the product exist in Israel? Is Studio Dermal (or the same white-label product) sold or advertised in Israel?

### Takeaway
The category exists in Israel, but it is thin, fragmented and carried mostly by big foreign brands. Three recognisable instant / temporary eye-tightening products have Israeli sellers: Peter Thomas Roth Instant FIRMx Eye (₪198 to ₪199), L'Oréal Paris Revitalift Laser "gel-to-tape" under-eye cream (₪85.90 to ₪110, in the pharmacy chains) and Jeunesse Instantly Ageless (₪310 to ₪315, through network-marketing distributors). In 29 searches nothing turned up for Studio Dermal, for TMP-02's white-label formula, for an Israeli Plexaderm seller or for a Plexaderm-style Hebrew dropshipping funnel. Hebrew search results for the literal product terms are dominated by machine-translated amazon.com listings.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Israel is not virgin territory. The instant-eye promise has been sold here since at least 2017 through Jeunesse network marketing (launch posts dated 2017/05 and 2017/09/12, section 4). L'Oréal later brought it into mass retail. Still, no Hebrew direct-to-consumer brand owns the category, so the slot a Studio Dermal-type seller would take (Hebrew D2C brand, about ₪100 to ₪170, sold through Meta ads) looks empty in this run's evidence. That evidence comes from search snippets and is incomplete.
- [INFERENCE] Hebrew results for the literal terms are filled by machine-translated amazon.com listings, so an Israeli seller could probably win Hebrew organic search for these terms with a proper Hebrew landing page. "מהדק עיניים" looks like Amazon machine-translation vocabulary. Israeli sellers instead write "מתיחה מיידית" [instant lift / stretch], "מיצוק מיידי" [instant firming], "אפקט סינדרלה" [Cinderella effect] and "שקיות מתחת לעיניים" [under-eye bags] (sections 2 and 4).
- [INFERENCE] Israel Hayom's negative test of L'Oréal's gel-to-tape cream is a headwind for the whole category. Readers may conclude that "instant creams do nothing", so unfiltered before/after video and live demonstrations will probably matter more in Israel than in the UK or US.
- [INFERENCE] The "Cinderella effect" framing ("ולו לכמה שעות" [if only for a few hours]) shows that Israeli beauty media already accept a temporary, event-driven benefit. That fits the TMP-02 promise if it is framed honestly as temporary.
