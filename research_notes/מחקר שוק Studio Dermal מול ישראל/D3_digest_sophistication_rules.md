## 1. Source-market sophistication

**Verdict: US = Schwartz Level 4 tipping into 5; UK = Level 4 with Level 5 pockets in DTC social ads** [INFERENCE from verified evidence, A7]. All five stages are documented: a simple claim, enlarged speed and duration numbers, new mechanisms (peptide "Botox in a bottle", "shale clay ... invisible shield", silicate "liquid glass"), refined execution (tint, "Easy Wear", "advanced peptide complex"; undated, PureWow and [Niche Beauty](https://www.niche-beauty.com/en-us/produkte/peter-thomas-roth-instant-firmx-eye-temporary-eye-tightener-easy-wear/192-194)), then distrust. Buyers accept that a temporary effect exists; skepticism targets duration, residue, safety and sellers: "doesn't last long enough or that a residue is left behind" ([MakeupAlley, result set](https://makeupalley.com/product/showreview.asp/ItemId=175907/Instantly-Ageless/Unlisted-Brand/Treatments-(Eye))) [VERIFIED]; "Sodium silicate, also known as liquid glass" ... "highly alkaline ingredients like sodium silicate can be damaging to the skin" ([The Derm Review](https://thedermreview.com/instantly-ageless/)) [VERIFIED], a critique aimed at TMP-02's own mechanism [INFERENCE].

**Implications** [INFERENCE, A7]:
- Israel: A7 expects an effective Level 3-4 audience (local ad history near Level 2-3, plus the same global demos and debunks); B4 and B7 hold no Israeli evidence on this.
- Show, do not claim: a single-take, unfiltered, timestamped demo with "temporary" first; pre-empt residue, cracking and wear time only where tested; frame by occasion and identity; make trust visible (real price, no subscription, all reviews).
