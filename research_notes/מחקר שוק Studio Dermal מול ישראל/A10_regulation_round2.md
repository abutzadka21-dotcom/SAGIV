Prepared 2026-10-04 for the red-lines tables of the Studio Dermal vs Israel market report. Scope: Meta, TikTok and Google ad policies (global, so they also apply in Israel); UK (ASA/CAP, CMA, DMCC Act 2024); US (FTC, FDA, MoCRA); fake urgency and price anchoring.

**Research conditions and conventions (read first)**
- WebSearch only (WebFetch is blocked in this environment; no page was opened). Budget for this round: max 35 searches. Running count is logged in "Search log" at the end.
- "Tool text:" means text copied exactly as the search tool returned it. The search tool writes its own summary of pages, so tool text may be the tool's paraphrase of the page rather than the page's own words. Where the tool itself put words inside quotation marks, I say so ("quoted by the tool"), which suggests page wording but is still not a guarantee.
- When the tool did not tie a sentence to one URL, I write "result set" and list the URLs it returned. I attach no URL that the tool did not return.
- Tags: [VERIFIED] = tied to a URL returned by the search tool this round. [INFERENCE] = my conclusion. [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE] (GK) = background with no source this round.
- Round-1 items already verified in A7 (category history, Hydroxatone, product claims of competitors) are not repeated.
