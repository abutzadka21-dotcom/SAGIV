**Definition of a cosmetic, and the line against drug claims**
- [VERIFIED, paraphrase] Under the Pharmacists Ordinance, a cosmetic is any substance or mixture intended to come into contact with external parts of the human body, with the primary or exclusive purpose of cleaning, perfuming, changing its appearance, protecting or maintaining it, or improving body odours, and excluding pharmaceutical preparations and medical devices. Result set: [Nevo, פקודת הרוקחים [נוסח חדש], תשמ"א-1981](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/p211_002.htm); [Hebrew Wikisource text of the Ordinance](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%97%D7%99%D7%9D). The tool's English rendering, not the statute's Hebrew wording.
- [VERIFIED, paraphrase] A cosmetic may not be presented as having a therapeutic effect without approval as a drug; claims such as "heals", "treats acne" or "prevents inflammation" are treated as medical claims. Result set of search 12: most likely [avivbarishuy.co.il, "משרד הבריאות מחלקת תמרוקים"](https://avivbarishuy.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%93-%D7%94%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D/) or [Pharmaline, "סיווג לקטגוריות רגולטוריות: ההבחנה בין תרופות לתמרוקים"](https://pharmaline.co.il/articles/150137/). Consultancy-level sources.
- [VERIFIED, paraphrase, older regime] Since its 1999 cosmetics licensing procedure, the MoH has required marketers of "specific-use cosmetics" (those meant for medical uses or credited with specific properties) to prove suitability for the stated purpose as well as safety; for "regular" cosmetics only ingredient safety was examined; for specific claims the registration holder had to attach the test protocol and results for MoH review. Result set: [MoH "דו"ח טיוב רגולציה - תמרוקים" (2015, per the PDF name) via chamber.org.il](https://www.chamber.org.il/media/154882/201520tamrukim.pdf); [Pharmaline, "רישום תמרוקים בישראל"](https://pharmaline.co.il/articles/150183/). **This describes the pre-2023 licensing regime;** I could not confirm how claims are handled under the 2023 notification track.
- [VERIFIED, paraphrase] The 2023 regulations contain provisions on labelling, usage instructions, warnings, usage limitations and disposal instructions, and ban dangerous substances. Source: [Nevo, regulations](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/215688.htm). A separate labelling-exemption regulation exists: ["תקנות הרוקחים (פטור מסימון תמרוק), תש"ע-2010"](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/73494.htm) (the tool linked it to products with a shelf life over 30 months).
- [VERIFIED, title only] A Hebrew class-action page on breaches of MoH rules: [fridmanwork.com, "הפרת הוראות משרד הבריאות - תביעה ייצוגית"](https://www.fridmanwork.com/lawyers40445.html). The Ordinance's administrative-fine version is on gov.il, title "פקודת הרוקחים (נוסח חדש)- עיצום כספי": [gov.il PDF](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/laws_authorizing_the_center_to_collect_debts/he/%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%97%D7%99%D7%9D%20(%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97%20%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9)-%20%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%9D%20%D7%9B%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%99.pdf). Fine amounts not returned.
- [VERIFIED, title only] MoH public warnings about specific cosmetic products happen, e.g. [ice, "משרד הבריאות מזהיר משימוש במוצר הפופולרי בישראל: "חשש לזיוף""](https://www.ice.co.il/health/news/article/1066002) (date and product not returned).

## Q2. Personal import: VAT exemption threshold (75 vs 150 USD), customs exemption, 2025-2026 changes

### Takeaway
As of the latest sources found (mid-2026), the VAT exemption on personal imports is **back at 75 USD**. The threshold went through two short-lived raises: a 150 USD order took effect on 24 December 2025 and was revoked by the Knesset plenum in February 2026 (59-25); a replacement 130 USD order took effect at midnight about 24/25 February 2026 and was revoked by the Knesset 59-23 on a Tuesday in spring 2026 (exact date not returned). The High Court did not block either order on its own. The customs-duty exemption ceiling is 500 USD per an undated guide. For a single 29.99 USD unit nothing changes: it is below 75 USD under every version.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] **For TMP-02 the threshold fight barely matters for a single unit.** A 29.99 USD order (plus shipping, which per the addressil page is excluded from the calculation) is under 75 USD, so a consumer buying directly from the brand, Amazon, AliExpress or Temu pays no Israeli VAT. A 3-unit bundle (about 90 USD) would exceed 75 USD and attract 18% VAT on personal import. A seller holding stock in Israel charges 18% VAT on every sale, so the price gap against direct cross-border buying is about 18% plus the seller's margin.
- [INFERENCE] **Policy volatility.** The threshold changed three times between December 2025 and spring 2026, and the Finance Minister twice tried to raise it. A further attempt after the October 2026 elections cannot be excluded. Price comparisons in the report should be dated.
- [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE, not verified] The Knesset election is scheduled for late October 2026; a new government could revisit the threshold.
