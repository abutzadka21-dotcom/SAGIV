**Import approvals and personal-use import**
- [VERIFIED, title only] gov.il service: ["בקשת אישור ייבוא (תמרוקים)"](https://www.gov.il/he/service/application-for-cosmetic-import-permits)
- [VERIFIED, paraphrase] Personal import means import by an individual, not through a business, where the goods are not meant for supply, manufacturing or services and come in a reasonable quantity for personal or family use. Cosmetics are regulated goods, so even a personal import may need regulatory approval depending on product type and quantity. Result set: [Prague Point, "מהם תמרוקים ואיך מייבאים אותם לישראל?"](https://praguepoint.co.il/how-to-import-cosmetics-to-israel/); [Ministry of Economy "צו יבוא אישי" page](https://apps.economy.gov.il/Apps/PersonalImport/Home/Digital_Order/)
- [VERIFIED, paraphrase, ambiguous] The tool said a "reasonable quantity" is defined as up to 30 units of the same type in a single shipment, for shipments valued up to 1,000 dollars. Most likely from the [Ministry of Economy "צו יבוא אישי" page](https://apps.economy.gov.il/Apps/PersonalImport/Home/Digital_Order/). The tool's sentence was garbled ("either ... or" with one branch missing), so treat the 30 units / 1,000 dollars pairing as unconfirmed.
- [VERIFIED, paraphrase] It is prohibited to import, manufacture or market cosmetics without the appropriate MoH approval; specific provisions allow personal import under conditions of quantity and intended use. Result set: [Prague Point](https://praguepoint.co.il/how-to-import-cosmetics-to-israel/); [GPC, "רישום תמרוקים וקוסמטיקה משרד הבריאות"](https://www.product-certification.com/%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%93-%D7%94%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA/%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%98%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94-%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%AA%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%93-%D7%94%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA/)
- [VERIFIED, title + paraphrase] A wartime relief for importing donated cosmetics was extended to 31.8.24; the tool's summary said such exemptions apply only to goods marketed in the EU, Britain, the USA and Canada and not intended for babies or pregnant women, and do not exempt the importer from other legal duties such as storage and transport conditions. Source: [chamber.org.il, "משרד הבריאות מאריך את מתווה להקלות ביבוא תרומות תמרוקים נוכח מלחמת חרבות ברזל עד ל-31.8.24"](https://www.chamber.org.il/foreigntrade/1109/1110/144846/). This is about donations, not sales.
